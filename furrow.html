      How to play

      1. Every day you get five prompts about Andre. Everyone gets the same five.
      2. Type one answer per prompt. You have 30 seconds, and whatever is in the box when time runs out is submitted.
      3. Get it exactly right for 100 points. Something close scores 70–90, something in the right area 40–60, and a miss 0–30.
      4. After each answer, Andre reveals his real answer and the story behind it.
      5. Finish all five to see your score out of 500, your rank, and a result card you can share.
      6. One run per day. New prompts arrive at midnight Toronto time.
